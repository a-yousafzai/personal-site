Selected Impact

Four stories that show how I lead — from prototype to scaled, paying-customer deployments. Drawn from the work I'm proudest of across Salesforce and Comcast.

SIEM · Detection & Response

Salesforce SIEM at TB/day scale

Context: CSOC needed a single ingestion plane spanning every Salesforce business unit, with strict MTTD/MTTR SLAs and constant pressure to lower signal-to-noise.

What I did: Took ownership of the SIEM platform within CSOC Engineering — ingestion, parsing, detection enablement, and 24/7 ops. Drove an SNR program to retire low-value sources and tighten high-value ones, and partnered with Detection Engineering to keep alert quality on a forward trend.

Result: Multiple terabytes/day flowing reliably across BUs; CSOC holding MTTD/MTTR SLAs while data quality climbs and noise drops.

Endpoint Security · Control Plane

Security control plane for 1.4M endpoints

Context: Custom security controls were fragmented across acquired business units — inconsistent coverage, slow rollouts, painful changes.

What I did: Centralized the security control plane for 1.4M endpoints, paired with a Change Management & Change Safety framework so large rollouts could land predictably. Managed the global team across US, Europe, and India that owns it.

Result: One control plane across the fleet; 1M+ endpoints migrated across 41 BUs through complex M&A transitions with no major outages.

Big Data · Security Intelligence

Home-grown big-data platform for SecOps

Context: Comcast SecOps needed log analytics and security-intelligence visualization that off-the-shelf tooling couldn't deliver at the org's volume and cost profile.

What I did: Architected the platform ground-up using Hadoop / Spark / Airflow patterns, partnered with Threat Modeling and PenTest, and wrapped it with a logging-onboarding workflow other teams could self-serve.

Result: Became the analytics backbone for Comcast global security operations; later extended into a Salesforce-side data platform for security health reporting and autonomous-remediation telemetry.

Operations · Automation

80% reduction in operational ticket volume

Context: The endpoint security team was spending more time on toil than on engineering — common asks were repeating themselves, week after week.

What I did: Drove a custom automation effort across the most frequent ticket categories, ran the team in Agile/Scrum, and protected eng time so the automation could actually ship.

Result: 80% drop in operational ticket volume and 20% increase in delivery throughput — without growing the team.
