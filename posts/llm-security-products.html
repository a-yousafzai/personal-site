What 10 years of SIEM taught me about building AI-powered detection

Draft · Coming soon

This post is in progress. The thesis: LLMs are fantastic at summarization, triage, and translation between humans and data — and consistently mediocre at being load-bearing in a detection pipeline. Use them where the failure mode is "a human reads it"; don't use them where the failure mode is "an alert is missed."

  • Why "agent triages every alert" is a worse pitch than it sounds

  • Building products humans trust without overselling autonomy
